What comes back

01Indicative fit

Likely NX configuration or combination, the critical IT basis, a preliminary utility and land requirement, and the workload and cooling assumptions behind it.

02Delivery basis

Earliest credible schedule range, where the clock starts and stops, the critical dependencies, and which of them belong to you, to us, to the utility or to a partner.

03Commercial next step

The engagement model available for this project, the information needed for a budgetary estimate, the NDA or diligence path, and a named Flexnode owner with a target response date.

What you will not receive is an instant engineered quote or a guaranteed date. Price depends on site, scope and responsibility boundary, and a number without a scope matrix is not comparable to anything.
