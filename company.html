Our story

Born from construction. Built for AI infrastructure.

Flexnode started from a conviction that the bottleneck in AI would be physical. Power, sites, engineering, supply chains, permitting, construction and operations. Seven years later that is the consensus. It was not in 2019.

01The idea

Manufacturing speed. Building adaptability.

Three approaches exist in this market. Centralized campuses, containerized appliances, and distributed platforms. Flexnode is the third.

A centralized campus is a multi-gigawatt, multi-year commitment in a handful of locations. A container is fast and cheap and cannot adapt, because it is welded. The middle was empty: a permanent building that behaves like a product.

People say they just want compute, and that building it is nothing. It is not nothing. It is a lot.

Andrew Lindsey, Founder and CEO

Where the method comes from

  • Complex building programsWork where repeatable subassemblies still had to adapt to each jurisdiction, each climate and each security requirement. That is the same problem, at a different scale.
  • Manufacturing and logisticsLess waste, more agility, a smaller footprint and a facility right-sized to its deployment, instead of stick-built and custom every time.
  • Compounding small failuresA lost manual, a missing document, a vendor phone number nobody has any more. At generational-buildout scale those become billions in stopped projects.
02Company timeline
2019

Founded

Flexnode is founded around modular, liquid-cooled infrastructure for high-density computing, years before liquid cooling became a mainstream requirement.

Thesis

High density would arrive faster than the buildings meant to house it.

2021

From thesis to a real site

Pre-commercial product development continues while the first documented site-feasibility work begins. Permitting, technical specification and project economics move the thesis into deployment planning.

  • Modular configurability and adaptability studied as the core product question
  • First feasibility, permitting and pro-forma analysis on a real parcel

Artifact

Feasibility and permitting studies. The first time the product met a jurisdiction.

2022

An industrial kit of parts

Design and manufacturing planning begins with Arup, Thornton Tomasetti, SHoP and JE Dunn to develop a manufacturing-ready modular data center platform.

  • Engineering, architecture and construction brought in at the design stage, not after it

Shift

The product stops being a building design and becomes a production system.

2023

Capital, federal validation and a prototype

First capital raised, led by strategic industry investors. Arup, JE Dunn and SHoP become invested design and delivery partners supporting design, construction and manufacturing development.

  • The US Department of Energy selects Flexnode for the ARPA-E COOLERCHIPS program
  • A liquid-cooled prototype is manufactured and tested in Long Island City with Boyce Technologies
  • Arup digital twin and operating-model work begins
2024

Out of stealth

The ARPA-E award formally begins: a $3.5 million, 36-month federal program for a prefabricated liquid-cooled micro data center. The kit-of-parts prototype debuts publicly at DCD NYC.

  • V1 design and prototype work completed
  • Modular design work with a hyperscaler begins, supporting a gigawatt-scale AI factory program
  • The Universal Building Platform is introduced as a scalable, configurable platform

Public record

$3.5M, 36 months, US Department of Energy ARPA-E COOLERCHIPS.

2025

From prototype to product

Independent third-party market, product-positioning and go-to-market work validates the commercial opportunity and focuses the company on the distributed inference band.

  • Platform V1 and a 3 MW high-density reference design
  • Prototype learning converted into a standardized product and customer-configuration roadmap

Focus

The market splits. Inference grows faster than training on power, capital and deployments.

2026

NX, and an Eaton-led Series A

Flexnode introduces NX as a standardized 4.5 to 18 MW product family and closes a Series A led by Eaton, strengthening both the capital position and the power-infrastructure base.

  • NX-1 through NX-4 published as a configuration family
  • First-build and customer-conversion work begins
Today

Applying seven years of it

Design, research, prototype and market learning applied to permanent AI data center buildings and a repeatable deployment method. The work now is turning a validated platform into commissioned capacity on real sites.
