02Conditions

What has to be true for an eight-month deployment.

The schedule is a consequence of these six conditions. Where one is missing we scope a longer path and say so in writing, rather than discovering it in month five.

Energized service, a contracted service agreement, or a utility commitment that lands before commissioning. Flexnode qualifies and fits available power. We do not create it, and we do not claim that any site avoids utility work.

Ownership or a controlling interest in the parcel, with a zoning basis that permits the use. A conditional-use path is workable. An unresolved rezoning is a different schedule.

Capacity, thermal strategy, redundancy and layout signed off. Configuration changes after production release move the date.

Long-lead items sit on the critical path. Customer-owned compute, network gear and any specified alternates need dates, not intentions.

The clock starts at binding commitment with funds released. A term sheet without mobilization is a plan, not a schedule.

Early contact with the authority having jurisdiction and the utility, run as a workstream with named owners rather than as correspondence.

Finish is defined as commissioned operational capacity, not modules delivered to site.
