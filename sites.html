02Status definitions

A megawatt on a map is not a megawatt at a meter.

Every listing we publish carries a status and a last-verified date. These four words mean four different things, and most disappointment in this market comes from treating them as one.

Energized

Service is live at the parcel. Capacity is metered and available now.

Contracted

A signed service agreement exists with a committed energization date.

Planned

In the utility plan or queue. Date is forecast, not committed.

Under diligence

Being verified. Published only with the open questions named.
