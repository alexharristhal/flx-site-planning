Community and environmental adaptability

Built to belong where it operates.

A data center is a building in a real place. Power, water, sound, traffic, land use and visual character are design and approval requirements from the first week, not a communications exercise in month nine.

01The questions communities are right to ask

Flexnode deploys where capacity already exists or is arriving within the project window. That is the whole premise of the configuration range: fit the building to the service, rather than requesting a new multi-year interconnection and waiting.

For any specific project we publish the service basis, the contracted load and the utility the agreement sits with. What a project does to local rates is a question for the utility and the regulator, and we will point you to both rather than answer for them.

NX is designed around closed-loop liquid cooling. The working fluid circulates and is rejected to air rather than evaporated, which is the mechanism behind a low operational draw.

Consumption is configuration and climate specific. We publish a modelled figure per project with the assumptions attached, and we do not publish a single headline number that would be wrong somewhere.

Heat rejection equipment runs continuously and is the dominant source. Backup generation runs during testing and outages, on a published schedule.

Sound is modelled to the property line before a hearing, against the local ordinance. Where the model exceeds the limit the response is acoustic enclosure, equipment selection and layout, and those are configuration decisions we can still make at that stage.

Construction traffic is concentrated and short. Modules arrive on a scheduled sequence over weeks rather than trades arriving daily for a year, which is a direct consequence of factory production.

Operating traffic is light: service visits, not shift changes. Facade, screening, planting and lighting are configured to the local context, and the elevation goes in the public record before the hearing.

Tax base, construction work, a reused parcel and, on some sites, infrastructure that outlasts the tenant. Those are the honest categories.

We do not publish job numbers we cannot evidence. A distributed facility of this scale is not a large permanent employer, and saying otherwise is how this industry loses arguments it should win.

03The approval path

Identify the approval path before it becomes the critical path.

Five gates, each with a go or no-go. The purpose is to reduce late surprises and build a credible path. No one bypasses process, and no one guarantees a permit.

  • Screen zoning and land useIs the use permitted, conditional or a rezoning? This single answer separates an eight-month project from a two-year one.
  • Map environmental and code requirementsWhat triggers review in this jurisdiction, which agency runs it, and the honest duration.
  • Engage the utility and the AHJEarly, named, and run as a workstream with dates rather than as correspondence.
  • Model sound, water, traffic and visual impactBefore the hearing, against the local ordinance, with the assumptions published.
  • Prepare public and permit documentationA fact sheet a neighbour can read, and a submittal set a reviewer can approve.
